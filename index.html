01 The stop is part of the journey.

How it works

Simple by design.
Meaningful by mile.

Pathfinder is designed to recognize repeat journeys without turning every stop into another transaction to manage.

  1. 01

    Join at no cost

    Enroll when the program becomes available. Explorer status begins with your first qualifying charging session.

  2. 02

    Charge with Rangeway

    Qualifying sessions and energy use build toward the next Pathfinder tier across participating locations.

  3. 03

    Unlock recognition

    Higher tiers are planned to add charging credits, destination perks, experiences, and more personal service.

02 More than a charging session.

The program

Reward the journey,
not just the transaction.

Charging keeps you moving. Hospitality is what makes you want to return.

Pathfinder is being built around earned recognition: a free-to-join program where activity, not a paid subscription, advances your status.

  • One base charging rate for every guest
  • Earned status through qualifying activity
  • Useful benefits rooted in the destination
  • Human recognition at every tier

Membership tiers

Four ways to be
recognized.

The current program concept uses annual charging sessions or energy delivered to determine status. Reach either threshold first.

01Entry

Explorer

Begins with your first qualifying session

Member access

  • Digital member status
  • Member communications and offers
  • Progress toward earned status
02Frequent

Voyager

10–24 sessions or 3,000+ kWh annually

5% charging credit

  • Explorer benefits
  • Planned birthday recognition
  • Select destination and partner perks
03Elite

Pioneer

25–49 sessions or 7,500+ kWh annually

10% charging credit

  • Voyager benefits
  • Planned annual recognition
  • Enhanced destination experiences
04By invitation

Trailblazer

Reserved for exceptional community contribution

15% charging credit

  • Pioneer benefits
  • Exclusive gatherings and previews
  • More personal recognition

Working program concept. Thresholds, charging credits, benefits, qualifying activity, status duration, and launch timing remain subject to change.
