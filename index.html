How it works

Simple by design.
Meaningful by mile.

Pathfinder is designed to recognize repeat journeys without turning every stop into another transaction to manage.

  1. 01

    Join at no cost

    Enroll when the program becomes available. Explorer status begins with your first qualifying charging session.

  2. 02

    Charge with Rangeway

    Qualifying sessions and energy use build toward the next Pathfinder tier across participating locations.

  3. 03

    Unlock recognition

    Higher tiers are planned to add charging credits, destination perks, experiences, and more personal service.

The program

Reward the journey,
not just the transaction.

Charging keeps you moving. Hospitality is what makes you want to return.

Pathfinder is being built around earned recognition: a free-to-join program where activity, not a paid subscription, advances your status.

  • One Every guest pays the same base charging rate.
  • Earned Status advances through qualifying activity.
  • Useful Benefits are rooted in the destination.
  • Human Recognition is built into every tier.

Membership tiers

Four ways to be
recognized.

The current program concept uses annual charging sessions or energy delivered to determine status. Reach either threshold first.

01Entry

Explorer

Begins with your first qualifying session

5% charging credit

  • Session history and receipts
  • Start/stop notifications
  • Charging dashboard and member communications
02Frequent

Voyager

25 sessions or 2,500 kWh annually

10% charging credit

  • Premium beverage at Basecamps
  • Birthday credit and trip-planning access
  • Select partner offers and early-location access
03Elite

Pioneer

50 sessions or 5,000 kWh annually

20% charging credit

  • Lounge upgrade and reserved seating
  • Two guest passes and member-event access
  • Priority charging reservations
04By invitation

Trailblazer

Reserved for exceptional community contribution

50% charging credit

  • Black Card kit and relationship manager
  • VIP lounge access and unlimited guest passes
  • Preview access and a voice in the program

Working program concept. Thresholds, charging credits, benefits, qualifying activity, status duration, and launch timing remain subject to change.

Member experience

Benefits that belong
to the place.

Pathfinder’s planned benefits extend beyond the plug. The aim is to make the time spent charging more useful, more comfortable, and more connected to the route.

01

Charging credits

Earned credits are planned to return value toward future qualifying sessions.

02

Destination perks

Select locations may include food, drink, lounge, retail, or local partner benefits.

03

Recognition

Tier milestones, member moments, and a warmer welcome for repeat guests.

04

Community access

Planned invitations to previews, gatherings, and experiences along the network.
